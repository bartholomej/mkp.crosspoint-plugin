Czech E-books (MKP)

Free e-books of the Municipal Library of Prague (Městská knihovna v Praze) on a CrossPoint reader. Unofficial.

The books come from the library's catalog of free e-books: https://www.mlp.cz/katalog/davka/NFW-EKNIHY-VOLNE

Open Home > Plugins > Czech E-books (MKP), pick a list, then a title to see its details and download it. To search, open any list and press Search on its first row.

Books are saved to /MKP as "Author - Title.epub". Titles available only as PDF are hidden.
